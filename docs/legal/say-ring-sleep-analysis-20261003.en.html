Say Ring Sleep AI Analysis Notice

Version: say-ring-sleep-ai-2026-10-03-v1. Date: 3 October 2026. This notice applies only to optional sleep AI analysis in Say Ring, not to Saydian Health consent.

1. Optional analysis

This feature generates a reference sleep score, analysis and everyday routine suggestions from the sleep record you select. It is not a medical service, diagnosis, treatment recommendation or clinical assessment.

You can read this notice and agree or cancel before each upload. Declining does not prevent connecting a ring or viewing its local sleep timeline. Guest mode does not upload sleep data for this feature.

2. Data uploaded

The upload contains the selected SDK date, recorded time zone, effective sleep duration, actually returned stage totals, and the start/end times and effective durations of night sleep and naps.

Device scores, wake counts and unknown/not-worn durations are included only if actually available. Missing values remain unknown. No fixed score substitutes for AI output.

Raw stage-by-stage timelines are not uploaded by this feature. The server stores necessary device and content hashes for record association and duplicate prevention; these hashes are not sent to AI.

3. Recipients and purpose

The Say Ring personal information controller is Xuewu Tang. Uploaded summaries and generated reports are stored with your cloud account and can be read by you and authorized health-report administrators.

The current third-party AI service is Zhipu BigModel, operated by 北京智谱华章科技股份有限公司. The Say Ring server calls its GLM models via HTTPS to generate analysis and suggestions for this sleep record.

AI receives only the listed sleep aggregates, session times and analysis instructions, not your name, phone, email, avatar, account ID, device UUID/MAC or raw timeline. Removing identity fields is not a guarantee of irreversible anonymization.

The provider also receives necessary server network information and processes input/output under its applicable rules. We do not promise zero retention or exclusion from all model improvement. Read the BigModel privacy policy: https://docs.bigmodel.cn/cn/terms/privacy-policy.

This feature sends data only to the disclosed service; changing recipients requires an updated notice and corresponding configuration.

4. Limits and risks

Sleep records reveal health and daily routine information. Cloud and third-party processing add handling stages; disclosure or inappropriate linkage remains a risk even when direct identifiers are removed.

Wearable stages are estimates, affected by wear, missing data and time settings. AI may be wrong. Its reference score is not a device or clinical score and cannot establish or exclude illness. One day's record does not establish a long-term trend; insufficient evidence may result in no score.

5. Withdrawal and deletion

Choose “Withdraw sleep AI analysis consent” on the report page to stop future sleep analysis. A result is not published if authorization changes during generation. Withdrawal does not change Health App consent or automatically delete previously generated reports.

Contact kf@saydian.com to request access, correction or deletion of uploaded summaries and reports. Closing this feature cannot instantly recall data already transmitted to the provider; requests are handled under applicable policies and rules.

6. Explicit confirmation

Selecting the upload-consent checkbox and then “Upload and analyze” authorizes this upload and provision of the listed data to the named provider for this analysis. Cancelling or leaving the box unchecked does not submit a generation request. A notice version change requires confirmation again.
