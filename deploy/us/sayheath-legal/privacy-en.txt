SayHeath Privacy Policy
Hong Kong Saydian Technology Limited operates SayHeath. This policy explains what happens when you sign in, connect a device, or share a category with a family member.

Account and storage. Your email, account identifier, sign-in and consent records are processed to create or access your account. Passwords are verified against a password hash. SayHeath uses a shared United States account service. Accepting this policy and the terms lets you see the activity and sleep history already associated with your own account. SayHeath's cloud records are stored on servers in the United States. Your iPhone also stores access credentials and, when necessary, records awaiting confirmation.

Device and health data. When you choose to connect a wearable, the app uses Bluetooth to identify the device and exchange supported data. Steps and sleep summaries can sync to your account. Where available, you choose separately whether to use blood pressure, blood oxygen, heart rate, pulse insights and scheduled blood pressure. Only new blood pressure, blood oxygen and heart rate readings collected through SayHeath after your permission may be sent to your US account. An older reading from another app is not imported as a new measurement. A pending upload is not a confirmed cloud record. Pulse history and device-reported indices are encrypted on your iPhone and are not uploaded. A blood pressure schedule is written to the wearable only after your confirmation; a schedule may continue on the wearable after disconnection. You can withdraw permission and stop a schedule on a connected, supported device.

Family Care. You decide whether to share activity, sleep, or both. An invitation must be accepted in SayHeath before access begins. Each shared category can be withdrawn separately, and either person can end the relationship. The recipient cannot use Family Care to view your blood pressure, oxygen, heart rate, pulse indices or schedule. Changes stop future access; they cannot erase information another person has already seen.

Support and retention. When you contact support, we process the reply address and message you provide. Do not include health values in support messages unless necessary. We use the information described here to provide and secure the requested services. We do not use these health records for advertising or sell them. Permissions and local sign-in credentials can be managed from the app. Signing out is not account deletion. In Account, choose Delete account to request deletion of the shared account, including access from other services that use it. Deletion is scheduled after seven days; legal obligations may require retention of limited records. You may also contact us through the SayHeath Support page about access, correction or deletion. We will publish material changes and request new consent where needed.
